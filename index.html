Check FSBuy product links

Open the original seller listing and save the marketplace name, item ID, exact colorway, and selected size. A Weidian product link, Taobao listing, or 1688 seller page may display several variants under one title. For an out-of-stock listing, look for the same model elsewhere and compare the variant details before updating your shortlist.

FSBuy sizing guide

For shoes, use your measured foot length and the seller’s sneaker size chart. For a hoodie or T-shirt, measure the chest width and body length of a garment you already own. For a jacket, compare shoulder width and sleeve length with room for layers. Check inseam and waist measurements for pants, and head circumference for fitted caps.

Review FSBuy QC photos

Ask for warehouse inspection photos of your exact item when that service is available. For shoes, compare both heels, toe boxes, and outsole edges. For jerseys, check print alignment and collar stitching. For accessories, ask for a close-up of the clasp, buckle, or zipper. A ruler photo can help when the listing leaves a measurement unclear.

Estimate FSBuy shipping costs

Record the item price, domestic shipping fee, and any agent service charge. For the international leg, compare actual parcel weight with volumetric weight under the chosen carrier’s rules. Use your destination country and postal code to request a quote. Include applicable import taxes or duties when comparing delivered totals.

Style references

Editorial images for comparing silhouette, fabric, and hardware. Check availability and item details in the seller listing.

FSBuy spreadsheet FAQ

What is an FSBuy spreadsheet?

It is a category-based index of product links that shoppers can use to build a shortlist and compare listings before ordering.

Where do the category links go?

All ten category links open HipobuyFind. To check agent compatibility, open the original listing and confirm that your chosen agent can handle that link and variant.

What should I look for in QC photos?

Check that the photos show the selected size and color, then inspect the areas that matter for the item, such as both shoe heels, garment seams, or a bracelet clasp.

Can I estimate shipping from the item price?

You need a parcel quote for your destination. Check the packed weight and dimensions, then account for any applicable handling fees, taxes, and duties separately.
